QUICK REFERENCE RANGES
Cadence: 170~185 SPM is a common target range. GCT: under ~240ms is generally efficient. Flight time: roughly 30~45% of cycle time. Stride length: about 0.5~0.75m (single step) for recreational runners. These are general guidelines, not strict rules — individual body types vary.
ELITE VS RECREATIONAL RUNNERS
Cadence: elite ~180~200 vs recreational ~155~175 SPM. GCT: elite ~150~200 vs recreational ~250~300ms+. Flight time: elite ~35~45% vs recreational ~15~25%. This app's colors follow the recreational scale (170+/160+, ≤270/≤300, 22%+/12%+).
UNDERSTANDING BALANCE METRICS
Cadence/GCT/Gyro Balance compare left vs right leg as an L:R ratio (50:50 = perfectly even). A 5~8 point gap is normal; 10+ persistently may signal a muscle imbalance worth checking.
UNDERSTANDING LEAN ANGLE
LEAN ANGLE shows your forward lean, plus total lean and lateral sway alongside. Forward lean: 4-8° good, 8-12° borderline, 12°+ excessive. Lateral sway: under 5° fine, 10°+ may signal weak core/hip stability.
WARM-UP & COOL-DOWN
Start with 5~10 min of light jogging or dynamic stretching to raise your heart rate gradually. After running, walk and stretch for 5~10 min to help recovery and reduce muscle soreness.
HYDRATION & FUELING
For runs over 60 minutes, drink small amounts of water regularly. For runs over 90 minutes, consider light carbohydrate intake (gels, bananas, sports drinks).
REST & RECOVERY DAYS
Muscles rebuild during rest. Beginners should take at least 1~2 full rest days per week, and avoid running through persistent joint pain.
INJURY WARNING SIGNS
Sharp, localized, or one-sided pain that worsens during a run is a warning sign. Stop running and rest; if pain persists more than a few days, consult a medical professional.
HEART RATE & ZONES
Heart rate zones help you train at the right intensity. Zone 1~2 (50~70% max HR) builds aerobic base and aids recovery. Zone 3 (70~80%) improves cardiovascular efficiency. Zone 4~5 (80~100%) develops speed and power but requires adequate recovery time. Max HR is roughly estimated as 220 minus your age.
